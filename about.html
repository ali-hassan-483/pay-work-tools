About

Pay & Work Tools is a small, independent site of free pay calculators for hourly, shift, freelance and marketplace workers in the US and UK.

It is run by a developer and SEO specialist. Tools are built one at a time, with the formula shown, a worked example, and sources dated on the page. No tool is published as a bare widget with no explanation.

Calculations run in your browser. The pay numbers you enter are not uploaded to, or stored by, this site. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact: alihassan7077984@gmail.com

Corrections: if you find a wrong rate, formula or source, contact the site owner with the page URL and the primary source. Rates are re-checked when tax and employment rules change, in January for US federal rules and April for the UK tax year.
