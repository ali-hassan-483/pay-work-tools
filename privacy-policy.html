Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Replace the contact placeholder on the About page before publishing, and update the advertising section below once you know exactly which ad and analytics code you have added.

Calculator data

The calculators on this site run entirely in your web browser (client-side JavaScript). The pay rates, hours and other numbers you type into a calculator are not sent to, collected by, or stored by this site. If you use the “Copy share link” button, the numbers are put into the link itself (in the page address) so you can share them — do not share that link if you do not want the recipient to see those numbers.

Analytics

If Google Analytics (GA4) or Google Search Console is added, it will collect standard, aggregated visit data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). No pay data you enter is sent to analytics. If you do not add analytics code, no analytics data is collected by this site itself.

Advertising — Adsterra

This site is monetised with Adsterra display advertising. Adsterra and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure them. Ad content is served by Adsterra, not by this site, and this site does not control the content of individual ads. Before publishing, check Adsterra’s own privacy and cookie information in your Adsterra account and link to it here if required. If you add only banner/native code, say so here. If you later add popunder or Social Bar code, update this section to describe it honestly.

Cookies

This site itself sets no cookies for the calculators. Advertising and analytics providers, if added, may set their own cookies. Your browser settings can block or delete cookies.

For privacy questions, use the contact email on the About page (replace the placeholder before publishing).
