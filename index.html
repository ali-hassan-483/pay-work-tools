How these tools are built

  • One job per page. No mega-list of unrelated tools.
  • Show the working. Formula, breakdown and a worked example on every tool page, not just a total.
  • Dated sources. Tax, employment and fee rules are checked against primary sources and dated. Rules change — re-check before you rely on a result for a dispute.
  • Estimate only. Results are planning estimates, not legal, tax or payroll advice. See the disclaimer.
